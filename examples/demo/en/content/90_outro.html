Three one-liners to remember

  1. Logistic regression is a linear classifier — its decision boundary is a hyperplane (P63).
  2. It learns parameters by maximum conditional likelihood — no closed form, but the objective is concave, so gradient methods reach the global optimum (P63).
  3. NB and LR make different assumptions — NB models ; LR assumes a functional form for and makes no assumption about (P63).
