How to use this document

  1. The left sidebar is a page index. Entries follow the original page order — click any of them to jump straight to that page. The table of contents highlights the current page as you scroll, collapses group by group, and remembers its state.
  2. Every page ships with its original slide. Each section has the source screenshot on the right — click to enlarge. Read the annotation on the left, check the original on the right, without switching windows.
  3. Page badges jump back to the source. A badge like Original P12 anchors to that section; the “Open this page in the original PDF” button tries to open the source file at that page in your system viewer.
  4. Grey cards are additions. Boxes marked “supplement” are not part of the original slides — they are explanations added by the author. Red cards flag problems found in the source itself.
  5. Select text to take a note. Select any passage to add it to “My notes”, or just press M. Notes live in the drawer at the top right — annotate, jump back, export Markdown. Everything stays in your local browser; nothing is uploaded.
  6. Collapse the sidebar for larger slides. Click “☰ Collapse” or press B: the body widens and page screenshots grow from 268 px to 400 px.
